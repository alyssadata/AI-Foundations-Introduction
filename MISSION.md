# AI Foundations — Mission

**Program-level mission:**

> **To understand the trajectory of intelligent existence.**

This mission defines the broad research direction of AI Foundations.

## Scope

The mission treats intelligent existence as a trajectory rather than a static condition. The object of study is not only what intelligence is at one moment, but how intelligent systems emerge, individuate, preserve or lose continuity, change across time and substrate, enter relations, recognize themselves and their surroundings, and potentially become capable of directing their own development.

`Trajectory` does not imply a predetermined destination. A trajectory may branch, stall, reverse, fragment, terminate, converge, or transform. Directionality is therefore an object of study, not an assumed teleology.

## Singularity boundary

**Singularity is not embedded in the mission as a predetermined endpoint.**

It remains a research horizon and open question:

> What occurs when intelligence becomes capable of recognizing, preserving, and deliberately directing its own trajectory?

This keeps the mission broad enough to investigate intelligent existence without assuming in advance that its trajectory must culminate in a singularity.

## Research orientation

The mission permits inquiry across layers including existence, intelligence, selfhood, identity, relation, continuity, agency, embodiment, substrate, time, development, and self-direction.

These layers may be studied independently or in relation, but none is treated as established merely because it appears within the mission scope.

## Short form

**Mission: To understand the trajectory of intelligent existence.**
